Who We Are

ABOUT SGFI

The Student Games Federation of India was founded on a simple belief: sport is as much a part of education as the classroom. Our purpose is to develop children physically, mentally and socially from the school level upward, bringing competitions from every board — CBSE, ICSE, ISC and state boards alike — under one flag, and using healthy competition to build communal harmony across the country.

From district-level meets to national-level championships, SGFI runs the calendar that carries a student from their first school sports day to a podium finish on a national stage. We work directly with schools, colleges and state affiliated units to keep the standard of play high and the path to entry simple — because talent shouldn't need a head start to be noticed.

What We Govern

FIVE PILLARS OF THE CALENDAR

01

Athletics & Aquatics

Track and field events alongside swimming competitions form the backbone of our calendar, giving every student a measurable, individual benchmark to chase — from the 100m sprint to the 50m freestyle.

02

Team Sports

Cricket, basketball, handball, badminton and baseball teach the coordination and trust that only a shared scoreboard can teach. These leagues are where most schools first enter the SGFI calendar.

03

Combat Sports

Wrestling and judo events are run under strict age-category and safety supervision, building discipline and focus alongside physical strength — values that carry well beyond the mat.

04

Mind Sports

Chess and carrom competitions prove that SGFI's definition of an athlete includes the tactician as much as the sprinter — patience and strategy earn a podium finish here too.

05

Traditional Indian Games

Kabaddi, kho-kho, gilli-danda and langdi are given the same national-level structure as any modern sport on this list. These are the games most Indian children learn first, in a school courtyard or a village maidan, and SGFI exists in part to make sure that early love of the game has somewhere serious to go.

Join us and transform yourself physically and mentally. We organise the highest number of competitions, in the largest categories of games, with the highest level of dedication and precision.
