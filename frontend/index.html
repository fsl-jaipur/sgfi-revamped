About SGFI

DEVELOP THE CHILD — PHYSICALLY, MENTALLY, SOCIALLY

SGFI exists to develop children physically, mentally and socially from the school level upward, bringing competitions from CBSE, ICSE, state boards and more under one flag and building communal harmony through sport across the country.
