STUDENT PREMIER LEAGUE

SGFI's flagship inter-zone cricket tournament, played out across six regional teams.

The Student Premier League is SGFI's flagship tournament — a T15 cricket competition where the country's best school and college talent plays under one set of regional colours instead of one school jersey. Six zonal teams, drawn from players across our affiliated units, compete over a five-week season for the SPL trophy.

Selection happens through zonal trials run by each affiliated unit; players who register for cricket through the SGFI registration form become eligible for trial call-ups in their region.

How It Works

TOURNAMENT FORMAT

Stage 1

Group Stage

All six zonal teams play each other once in a single round-robin group, with two points for a win and none for a loss. Net run rate breaks ties.

Stage 2

Playoffs

The top four teams from the points table advance to knockout semi-finals, played at neutral affiliated-unit grounds.

Stage 3

Final

Semi-final winners meet in the SPL Final, with the trophy and individual awards presented on the same day.
